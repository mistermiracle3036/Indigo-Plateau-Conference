The wider Brendan, Dawn, May, and Pokémon Stadium sheets contain additional
variants. This test build uses the leftmost/default variant from each sheet.
Bea and Mina use the documented subset of their larger walking-pose grids.
SirWhibbles' Kanto sheet supplies complete battle and overworld sets for
Lorelei and Agatha. His Team Rocket sheet supplies Giovanni, the original GSC
Rocket Executive, Archer, Ariana, Proton, Petrel, and the male and female
Rocket Grunts. The MOLLY miscellaneous grid supplies battle
portraits only, so Jessie & James and Oak use vanilla Gold-cache overworld art.
Roxie's battle and overworld sources are credited separately because they are
by different artists; the overworld conversion is the user-confirmed editable
UlithiumDragon sheet.
Piers likewise combines two separately credited sources. Only his figure is
clipped from Drawnamu's complete gym-leader lineup; the other portraits remain
only in the preserved evidence sheet.

The miscellaneous grid is unlabeled. Jessie, James and Meowth are visually
unambiguous; the selected Oak-like lab-coat cell is a device-test candidate
that must be confirmed before public release.

The announcer and every unlisted character use art from the player’s own
imported Pokémon Gold cache; no ROM-derived art is included in this mod.
