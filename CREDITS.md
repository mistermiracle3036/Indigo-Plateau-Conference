# Credits

**Indigo Plateau Conference** is by **Mister Miracle**
([@mistermiracle3036](https://github.com/mistermiracle3036)).

Built on the [gen1recomp](https://github.com/bryanthaboi/gen1recomp)
engine, with reference to the [pret/pokered](https://github.com/pret/pokered)
disassembly research.

## Character art

Art for the Conference roster is contributed by members of the
gen1recomp community. Contributors were asked for, and agreed to, these
terms when submitting:

- Contributed freely, with no payment expected or offered.
- Open source, and reusable by other gen1recomp modders **with credit**.
- Never sold, and never exclusive to this mod.

Each contributor chooses the name they are credited under. If you have
contributed art and want your credit name changed — or your work
removed — open an issue and it will be done.
