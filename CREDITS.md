# Credits

**Indigo Plateau Conference** is by **Mister Miracle**
([@mistermiracle3036](https://github.com/mistermiracle3036)).

> This file is the short, player-facing **art credit roll**. Licensing,
> scope and trademark notices live in
> [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md), which is the

> look for their name here, audits look for the notices there.

Built on the [gen1recomp](https://github.com/bryanthaboi/gen1recomp)
engine, with reference to the [pret](https://github.com/pret)
disassembly research.

## Character art

Art for the Conference roster is contributed by members of the
gen1recomp community. Contributors were asked for, and agreed to, these
terms when submitting:

- Contributed freely, with no payment expected or offered.
- Open source, and reusable by other gen1recomp modders **with credit**.
- Never sold, and never exclusive to this mod.

Each contributor chooses the name they are credited under. If you have
contributed art and want your credit name changed — or your work
removed — open an issue and it will be done.

**The mod ships no art of its own as of 0.9.7.** Every character on
screen wears a sprite that is already in the Gold ROM, referenced by its
id — nothing is copied out of the game and redistributed here.

The Announcer previously used original pixel art by Mister Miracle
(`assets/announcer.png`, drawn in Pixilart over a GridFab draft). It had
one frame and so never turned to face the player, and was replaced for
1.0.0 by `SPRITE_OLD_LINK_RECEPTIONIST`, which has the full standing set.
The art is not lost — it is in git history at `977661f` — and this row
comes back the moment the remaining frames are drawn.
